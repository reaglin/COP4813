COP4813

Module 14 — Content Management Systems, Webmaster Roles, and Site Maintenance

This module explores how Content Management Systems power modern websites, compares leading CMS platforms, and examines the strategic decision between a CMS and a custom build. It also defines the professional scope of the webmaster role and establishes best practices for ongoing website maintenance and production management.
